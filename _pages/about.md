Hi! I'm Andrea Gebek, a researcher in theoretical and numerical astrophysics at the [University of Bath](https://www.bath.ac.uk/departments/department-of-physics/) (UK).

I study how galaxies form and evolve over cosmic time using cosmological simulations. My work sits at the interface between simulations and observations: with radiative transfer calculations, I predict what simulated galaxies would look like through a real telescope. This makes direct, like-for-like comparisons with observational data possible.

I'm a member of the [COLIBRE](https://colibre.strw.leidenuniv.nl/) collaboration, which runs a large-volume suite of hydrodynamical simulations with a cold interstellar medium, and of the [SKIRT](https://skirt.ugent.be/root/_home.html) team, which develops the SKIRT radiative transfer code.

Research interests
------
- Dust radiative transfer and synthetic observations of simulated galaxies
- Resonant lines in galaxy outflows at cosmic noon
- Confronting simulations with observational surveys
- Testing SED fitting and other inference methods with simulated galaxies
